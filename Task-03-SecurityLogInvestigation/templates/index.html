EdVyro Defensive Lab Threat Model (6-Part Framework)

1. Vertical Brute-Force Password Guessing (T1110.001)

  • Asset & Owner: Account stu-117 and login service (Owner: University IT).
  • Trust Boundary & Entry: External ISP (198.51.100.27) to POST /api/v1/auth/login.
  • Scenario & Precondition: External actor tests passwords against stu-117. Precondition: Valid username known.
  • Existing Control & Evidence: Account locked after 3 failures in 34s (event logged at 09:05:03Z).
  • Risk Rating & Rationale: MEDIUM — Attack stopped, but lockout creates an unauthenticated DoS on victim.
  • Mitigation & Verification: Implement CAPTCHA after 2 failures; replace hard lockout with progressive 5m backoff.

2. Horizontal Password Spraying (T1110.003)

  • Asset & Owner: University student directory and central IAM store.
  • Trust Boundary & Entry: Untrusted proxy (203.0.113.42) to public login endpoint.
  • Scenario & Precondition: Attacker tests 1 common password across 4 accounts in 12s to evade account lockout thresholds.
  • Existing Control & Evidence: Edge rate limiter blocked 203.0.113.42 at 09:22:01Z.
  • Risk Rating & Rationale: CRITICAL — High probability of compromise if any student has a weak password.
  • Mitigation & Verification: Mandatory MFA; IP velocity limit (<2 accounts/5m per IP); drop unknown User-Agents.

3. Post-Spray Unusual Access Anomaly (T1078)

  • Asset & Owner: Academic records and session tokens of stu-311.
  • Trust Boundary & Entry: Internal Campus Wi-Fi (192.0.2.99) to portal application.
  • Scenario & Precondition: Account targeted in spray logs in successfully 145m later from campus on iOS.
  • Existing Control & Evidence: Password auth succeeded (HTTP 200). No MFA challenge logged.
  • Risk Rating & Rationale: HIGH — Requires out-of-band verification to separate legitimate student from attacker proxy.
  • Mitigation & Verification: Immediate session revocation; out-of-band identity challenge; campus Wi-Fi MAC audit.
